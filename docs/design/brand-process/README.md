# Brand design process

The boards we looked at, in order, while designing the Pelago identity. They record what was tried, what was chosen and what was rejected, so nobody repeats a rejected direction. The result is in [`../brand.md`](../brand.md) and `assets/brand/`.

## Round 1: the mark

| Board | What it shows | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [01-directions](01-directions.png) | Three directions: A islands, B wave P, C bar and dome. | B (a P with a wave) was the seed. |
| [02-variations](02-variations.png) | A1 smooth islands, A2 discs cut by a wave, A3 skyline, B1 P cut by a wave, B2 P in a badge, B3 monoline P with a wave. | B3 was loved. Monoline wins. |
| [03-refinements](03-refinements.png) | R1 to R6 on B3: heavy stroke, two waves, open bowl with a lighter wave, extra heavy, wave in the bowl, badge. | Lighter wave and open feel kept; island dot idea came back later. |

## Round 2: a drawn P against a font P

| Board | What it shows | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [04-font-p](04-font-p.png) | F1 to F6: Inter ExtraBold, JetBrains Mono, DejaVu Serif, Inter Display Light, Caladea, Liberation Sans. | Rejected: reads as developer-default or serif. |
| [05-custom-slab-p](05-custom-slab-p.png) | H1 to H6: custom slab P shapes. | Not chosen. |
| [06-serif-p](06-serif-p.png) | G1 to G6: serif P shapes. | "Stop with the serif P." Do not return to serifs. |
| [07-monoline-p](07-monoline-p.png) | J1 to J6: J1 base, J2 heavier, J3 flat ends, J4 open bowl, J5 slanted, J6 circle bowl. | **J1 won**: monoline capital P, round caps, dot right on the baseline, one wave. |
| [08-scenarios-j2](08-scenarios-j2.png), [09-scenarios-j6](09-scenarios-j6.png) | Finalists on light, dark, small sizes and as an app icon. | Confirmed J1 holds up better than J2 and J6. |
